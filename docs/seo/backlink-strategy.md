# Ovelo backlink strategy

This document is a prospective, ethical outreach plan. **No backlinks are claimed as acquired.** The goal is relevant discovery and useful references, not link volume.

## Priority channels

1. **Product and SaaS directories:** Submit a complete, accurate Ovelo profile to reputable directories that accept real productivity and household-organization products. Use the homepage and a concise description; avoid duplicate keyword-heavy submissions.
2. **Startup and product launch communities:** Prepare a transparent launch post for communities such as Product Hunt, Hacker News when technically relevant, and founder/startup communities where product launches are welcome. Participate in discussion rather than dropping a promotional link.
3. **Ownership and productivity communities:** Offer genuinely useful guides for household inventory, moving preparation, warranty organization, insurance-document preparation, and keeping records for shared homes. Respect community self-promotion rules and link only where it answers the question.
4. **Founder and technical writing:** Publish original explanations of private ownership records, secure QR scanning, authorization-scoped document storage, and the engineering tradeoffs of a small privacy-first product. Link to relevant How It Works or Privacy content.
5. **Original data and research:** If Ovelo obtains aggregated, consented, non-identifying usage data, publish useful research about warranty reminders, household record-keeping, or inventory habits. Do not expose user records or infer sensitive facts from private data.
6. **Integrations and partnerships:** Build useful, real integrations with moving services, repair networks, insurance-document workflows, storage providers, or privacy-focused productivity tools. Seek partner documentation pages and reciprocal references only when the integration is real.
7. **Legitimate guest contributions:** Contribute practical, non-duplicative articles to relevant personal-organization, home-management, privacy, and startup engineering publications. Use editorially appropriate links, not exact-match anchor-text campaigns.
8. **Open-source ecosystem:** Open-source narrowly reusable utilities, documentation, or security guidance when they are genuinely useful independently of Ovelo. Reference Ovelo only as the maintained product using the work.

## Quality rules

- Never buy links, use private blog networks, link farms, automated comments, spun articles, fake directory listings, or mass outreach.
- Prefer a small number of trusted, topical references over a high count of unrelated links.
- Use natural brand or descriptive anchor text such as “Ovelo ownership records” or “How Ovelo handles private records.”
- Keep outreach factual. Do not claim customers, awards, reviews, integrations, data, or press coverage that does not exist.
- Track prospect, contact, URL, relationship, requested page, and outcome without putting personal contact data or credentials in Git.

## Suggested first sequence

1. Finalize the public How It Works and Privacy pages.
2. Publish one genuinely useful moving/warranty-record guide on the Ovelo site.
3. Submit one accurate product-directory profile and one launch-community post.
4. Contact a small number of relevant integration partners with a real product use case.
5. Review referral quality and Search Console impressions monthly; stop channels that produce irrelevant traffic.
